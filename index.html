Pickup & no-show policy

Reserve it, then pick it up

Food only gets rescued if it leaves the counter. A reservation holds a portion nobody else can take, so missed pickups are tracked by student number. Nothing is ever charged for a no-show.

Clause 1

Cancel for free

Plans change. Cancel in My Reservations any time before your pickup window starts and it never counts against you. Cancelling after the window has started counts as a missed pickup.

Clause 2

Held until 30 minutes before close

Your portion is held until 30 minutes before the pickup window ends. If it hasn't been collected by then, it goes back on the app for walk-ups and counts as a missed pickup.

Clause 3

Missed pickups add up

  • 1 missA reminder the next time you reserve.
  • 2 in 30 daysYou can hold one reservation at a time.
  • 3 in 30 daysReserving is paused for 7 days. You can still buy at the counter.

Each missed pickup drops off your record 30 days after it happened. Check your record in My Reservations.
